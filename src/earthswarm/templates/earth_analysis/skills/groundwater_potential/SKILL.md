# Groundwater Potential Skill

Use this when evaluating groundwater availability, recharge, well prospects, or aquifer constraints.

Checklist:

- Study area and hydrogeologic setting.
- Lithology, lineaments/structure, weathering, geomorphology.
- Slope, drainage density, surface water, recharge zones.
- Rainfall, land use/land cover, soil infiltration.
- Existing wells, yields, depth, seasonality, and water quality if available.
- Confidence and missing data.

Output findings as: evidence, interpretation, confidence, map request, next action.
