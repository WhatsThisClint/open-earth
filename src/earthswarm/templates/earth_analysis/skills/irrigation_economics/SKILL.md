# Irrigation Economics Skill

Use this when evaluating water-related interventions, farm economics, livelihood impacts, or adoption feasibility.

Checklist:

- Beneficiaries and livelihood systems.
- Capital costs, O&M, labor, energy, and replacement costs.
- Crop or productivity benefit assumptions.
- Risk: rainfall variability, market price, water reliability, maintenance, governance.
- Distribution: who pays, who benefits, who bears risk.

Output: intervention, benefits, costs, assumptions, adoption risks, data needed.
