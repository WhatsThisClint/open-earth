# Environmental Risk Synthesis

Use this skill when combining specialist evidence into a decision-ready
environmental diagnosis.

## Synthesis Frame

For each issue, connect:

1. Driver: climate, land use, extraction, infrastructure, governance, or natural
   constraint.
2. Process: runoff, recharge, erosion, salinity, habitat loss, pollution,
   livelihood stress, etc.
3. Evidence: data layer, map, calculation, observation, or report.
4. Impact: water, soil, ecosystem, livelihood, infrastructure, equity.
5. Option: intervention, monitoring, governance action, or further data.
6. Risk: uncertainty, side effect, maintenance, conflict, cost, climate
   sensitivity.

## Output

Return a matrix with finding, evidence, confidence, implication, action, and
remaining uncertainty.
